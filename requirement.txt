How AQI is actually calculated
For India's CPCB methodology, each pollutant concentration is converted into an individual sub-index using pollutant-specific breakpoints.

The interpolation formula is:
\[
I_p=\frac{I_{HI}-I_{LO}}{B_{HI}-B_{LO}}
(C_p-B_{LO})+I_{LO}
\]

Where:
- \(I_p\) = Pollutant sub-index
- \(C_p\) = Measured pollutant concentration
- \(B_{HI}\) = Upper concentration breakpoint
- \(B_{LO}\) = Lower concentration breakpoint
- \(I_{HI}\) = Upper AQI breakpoint
- \(I_{LO}\) = Lower AQI breakpoint
The final AQI is:
\[
\boxed{AQI=\max(I_{PM2.5},I_{PM10},I_{NO2},\ldots)}
\]

In other words, the highest valid pollutant sub-index determines the overall AQI.
